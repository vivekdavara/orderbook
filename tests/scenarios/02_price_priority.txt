# A buy takes the lowest ask first, whatever order the asks arrived in.
> new 1 sell limit 5 @ 103
accepted 1
> new 2 sell limit 5 @ 101
accepted 2
> new 3 sell limit 5 @ 102
accepted 3
> new 4 buy limit 5 @ 103
accepted 4
trade 4 2 5 @ 101
= asks 102x5 103x5
> new 5 buy limit 6 @ 103
accepted 5
trade 5 3 5 @ 102
trade 5 1 1 @ 103
= asks 103x4
= bids

# A sell takes the highest bid first.
> new 6 buy limit 5 @ 97
accepted 6
> new 7 buy limit 5 @ 99
accepted 7
> new 8 buy limit 5 @ 98
accepted 8
> new 9 sell limit 7 @ 97
accepted 9
trade 9 7 5 @ 99
trade 9 8 2 @ 98
= bids 98x3 97x5
= asks 103x4
