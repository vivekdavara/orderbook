# Limit orders that don't cross rest on their own side, best price first.
> new 1 buy limit 10 @ 100
accepted 1
= bids 100x10
= asks
> new 2 buy limit 5 @ 101
accepted 2
= bids 101x5 100x10
> new 3 buy limit 7 @ 99
accepted 3
= bids 101x5 100x10 99x7
> new 4 sell limit 3 @ 103
accepted 4
> new 5 sell limit 8 @ 102
accepted 5
> new 6 sell limit 2 @ 104
accepted 6
= asks 102x8 103x3 104x2
= bids 101x5 100x10 99x7

# Orders at a price that already has orders join its level.
> new 7 buy limit 4 @ 100
accepted 7
= bids 101x5 100x14 99x7
> new 8 sell limit 1 @ 103
accepted 8
= asks 102x8 103x4 104x2
