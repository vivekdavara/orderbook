# Time priority on the bid side, and how it is lost: an order cancelled and sent again joins the back of the queue.
> new 1 buy limit 4 @ 50
accepted 1
> new 2 buy limit 4 @ 50
accepted 2
> new 3 buy limit 4 @ 50
accepted 3
> cancel 1
cancelled 1 4 requested
> new 4 buy limit 4 @ 50
accepted 4
= bids 50x12
> new 5 sell limit 10 @ 50
