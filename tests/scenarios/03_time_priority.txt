# Orders at one price trade in the order they arrived.
> new 1 sell limit 3 @ 100
accepted 1
> new 2 sell limit 4 @ 100
accepted 2
> new 3 sell limit 5 @ 100
accepted 3
= asks 100x12
> new 4 buy limit 5 @ 100
accepted 4
trade 4 1 3 @ 100
trade 4 2 2 @ 100
= asks 100x7

# Order 2, partly filled, keeps its place at the front: it trades before order 3.
> new 5 buy ioc 1 @ 100
accepted 5
trade 5 2 1 @ 100
= asks 100x6

# A new order at the same price joins the back of the queue.
> new 6 sell limit 2 @ 100
accepted 6
= asks 100x8
> new 7 buy limit 9 @ 100
accepted 7
trade 7 2 1 @ 100
trade 7 3 5 @ 100
trade 7 6 2 @ 100
= asks
= bids 100x1
