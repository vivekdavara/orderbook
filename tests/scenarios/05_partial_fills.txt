# A limit order that trades part of its quantity rests the rest at its own price.
> new 1 sell limit 3 @ 101
accepted 1
> new 2 buy limit 10 @ 101
accepted 2
trade 2 1 3 @ 101
= bids 101x7
= asks

# A maker can be filled by several takers; each trade takes from what is left.
> new 3 sell limit 2 @ 101
accepted 3
trade 3 2 2 @ 101
> new 4 sell limit 4 @ 100
accepted 4
trade 4 2 4 @ 101
= bids 101x1
> cancel 2
cancelled 2 1 requested
= bids
= asks
