# A limit order that crosses several levels takes them best first, then rests what is left at its own price, where
# it is now the best bid: the book is never left crossed.
> new 1 sell limit 2 @ 100
accepted 1
> new 2 sell limit 2 @ 101
accepted 2
> new 3 sell limit 2 @ 102
accepted 3
> new 4 sell limit 2 @ 104
accepted 4
> new 5 buy limit 1 @ 98
accepted 5
> new 6 buy limit 9 @ 102
accepted 6
trade 6 1 2 @ 100
trade 6 2 2 @ 101
trade 6 3 2 @ 102
= bids 102x3 98x1
= asks 104x2

# An order at exactly the best price on the other side crosses: equal prices trade.
> new 7 sell limit 1 @ 102
accepted 7
trade 7 6 1 @ 102
= bids 102x2 98x1
= asks 104x2
