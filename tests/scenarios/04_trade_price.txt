# Every trade is at the resting order's price, so a taker that crosses deeper than it needs gets the better price.
> new 1 sell limit 10 @ 100
accepted 1
> new 2 buy limit 4 @ 105
accepted 2
trade 2 1 4 @ 100
> new 3 buy limit 5 @ 95
accepted 3
> new 4 sell limit 2 @ 90
accepted 4
trade 4 3 2 @ 95
= bids 95x3
= asks 100x6

# IOC and market orders too.
> new 5 buy market 1
accepted 5
trade 5 1 1 @ 100
> new 6 sell ioc 1 @ 1
accepted 6
trade 6 3 1 @ 95
= bids 95x2
= asks 100x5
