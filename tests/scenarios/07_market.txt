# A market order trades at any price, best first, and cancels what the book can't fill.
> new 1 sell limit 2 @ 101
accepted 1
> new 2 sell limit 3 @ 150
accepted 2
> new 3 sell limit 4 @ 999999999
accepted 3
> new 4 buy market 6
accepted 4
trade 4 1 2 @ 101
trade 4 2 3 @ 150
trade 4 3 1 @ 999999999
= asks 999999999x3
> new 5 buy market 10
accepted 5
trade 5 3 3 @ 999999999
cancelled 5 7 unfilled
= asks

# Against an empty side there is nothing to trade with.
> new 6 sell market 1
accepted 6
cancelled 6 1 unfilled

# A market order has no price.
> new 7 sell market 1 @ 100
rejected 7 invalid-price
> new 7 buy limit 5 @ 1
accepted 7
> new 8 sell market 7
accepted 8
trade 8 7 5 @ 1
cancelled 8 2 unfilled
= bids
= asks
