# The checks run in order (the id, then the quantity, then the price), and the first that fails is the reason.
> new 0 buy limit 1 @ 100
rejected 0 id-not-increasing
> new 1 buy limit 0 @ 0
rejected 1 invalid-quantity
> new 1 buy limit 1 @ 0
rejected 1 invalid-price
> new 1 buy limit -1 @ 100
rejected 1 invalid-quantity
> new 1 buy limit 1000000001 @ 100
rejected 1 invalid-quantity
> new 1 buy ioc 1 @ 1000000001
rejected 1 invalid-price
> new 1 buy ioc 1 @ -100
rejected 1 invalid-price

# A rejected order leaves nothing behind, so its id can be used again.
= bids
= asks
> new 1 buy limit 1000000000 @ 1
accepted 1
> new 1 sell limit 1 @ 2
rejected 1 id-not-increasing
> new 2 sell limit 1 @ 1000000000
accepted 2
= bids 1x1000000000
= asks 1000000000x1
# An order that breaks all three rules is rejected for its id, and one with a valid id for its quantity.
> new 2 buy market 0 @ 5
rejected 2 id-not-increasing
> new 3 buy market 0 @ 5
rejected 3 invalid-quantity

# Ids must increase, but may skip.
> new 10 sell limit 1 @ 500
accepted 10
> new 9 sell limit 1 @ 500
rejected 9 id-not-increasing
> new 10 sell limit 1 @ 500
rejected 10 id-not-increasing
> new 11 sell limit 1 @ 500
accepted 11
= asks 500x2 1000000000x1
