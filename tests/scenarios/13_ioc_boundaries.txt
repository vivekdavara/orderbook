# An IOC crosses at exactly the other side's best price, and not a tick short of it.
> new 1 sell limit 5 @ 200
accepted 1
> new 2 buy limit 5 @ 190
accepted 2
> new 3 buy ioc 5 @ 199
accepted 3
cancelled 3 5 unfilled
> new 4 sell ioc 5 @ 191
accepted 4
cancelled 4 5 unfilled
= bids 190x5
= asks 200x5
> new 5 buy ioc 2 @ 200
accepted 5
trade 5 1 2 @ 200
> new 6 sell ioc 2 @ 190
accepted 6
trade 6 2 2 @ 190
= bids 190x3
= asks 200x3

# A market order stops only when it is filled or the other side runs out.
> new 7 sell market 100
accepted 7
trade 7 2 3 @ 190
cancelled 7 97 unfilled
= bids
= asks 200x3
