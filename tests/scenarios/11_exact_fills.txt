# A taker that exactly matches a maker fills both: the level goes, and nothing rests or is cancelled.
> new 1 buy limit 5 @ 100
accepted 1
> new 2 sell limit 5 @ 100
accepted 2
trade 2 1 5 @ 100
= bids
= asks

# A taker that exactly empties a level takes every order in it, in order.
> new 3 buy limit 3 @ 100
accepted 3
> new 4 buy limit 2 @ 100
accepted 4
> new 5 sell market 5
accepted 5
trade 5 3 3 @ 100
trade 5 4 2 @ 100
= bids

# So the side is empty again.
> new 6 sell ioc 5 @ 100
accepted 6
cancelled 6 5 unfilled
= bids
= asks
