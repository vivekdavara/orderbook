# An IOC order trades what it can at once, up to its price, and cancels the rest.
> new 1 sell limit 5 @ 101
accepted 1
> new 2 sell limit 5 @ 102
accepted 2
> new 3 buy ioc 8 @ 101
accepted 3
trade 3 1 5 @ 101
cancelled 3 3 unfilled
= asks 102x5
= bids

# One that doesn't cross at all is accepted, then cancelled whole: it never rests.
> new 4 buy ioc 5 @ 100
accepted 4
cancelled 4 5 unfilled
= bids

# One that fills completely has nothing left to cancel.
> new 5 buy ioc 2 @ 102
accepted 5
trade 5 2 2 @ 102
= asks 102x3

# Sells work the same way.
> new 6 buy limit 4 @ 99
accepted 6
> new 7 sell ioc 10 @ 99
accepted 7
trade 7 6 4 @ 99
cancelled 7 6 unfilled
= bids
= asks 102x3
